Book a Venue

Venues are given on a first come, first served basis. Submit your request at least 7 days before the event.

  • Main Auditorium - up to 500 people
  • Seminar Hall - up to 150 people
  • Cricket Ground - sports events
  • Open Lawn - cultural and social events

Event Rules

  • Every event needs approval from the event department.
  • Events must end by 9:00 PM unless special permission is given.
  • Organizers are responsible for the venue and its equipment.
  • Respect campus rules and keep the venue clean.
  • Posters must be approved before they are displayed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can organize an event?
Any registered student society.

How long does approval take?
Usually 3 to 5 working days.

Can I attend events from other departments?
Yes, all students are welcome.

Where do I submit my forms?
At the event department office or through the Contact page.
